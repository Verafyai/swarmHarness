## What differs from the scripted demo

- **fact-check-escalation passed live.** The scripted demo has it below the bar (3 of 5 agree, 60% against 70%). Live, all five models said `misleading`, so it passed at 100% against its 75% bar. The escalation path was not exercised by this run.
- **forecast went below the bar live.** The scripted demo seals it as `*forecast`. Live, the five estimates spread from 0.35 to 0.65, and agreement was 55% against a bar of 70%.
- **A rate limit dropped a harness.** One Google call hit an upstream 429 from the shared pool. The jury sealed with the four that answered. There is no retry yet; a retry or a provider-routing fallback would avoid the drop.
