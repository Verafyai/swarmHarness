# Proof of Judgment

*The record that makes a ruling checkable: who sat, what each committed and revealed, and the exit that ran.*

Proof of Judgment is the record every ruling leaves: the contract hash, the jury and how it was drawn, each juror's signed commitment and reveal, the agreement reached, and the exit with its exact transfers.

Anyone can re-check it. Signatures verify against each harness's card, reveals against commitments, the draw against the seed, and the transfers against the exit program.

It proves the process, not that the answer is true. That is what audits, challenges and, at the highest assurance level, proofs of inference are for.
