# The economics

*What a ruling costs, where the money goes, and why packing and routing matter.*

A ruling's cost is mostly model calls and, at L2, proofs. Every figure on the economics page is labeled: vendor-reported speeds and prices, modeled costs from `packages/economics`, or illustrative assumptions.

Headline numbers (modeled, H100 at $4.00 an hour):
- **Unpacked:** five jurors each proving: $0.1043 a decision.
- **Packed:** atomized, packed, proven once: $0.0059.
- **With a fast screen,** at assumed settle rates: $0.0039.

Once proofs are packed, the fidelity checks cost more than the proofs, which is where the next savings are.
